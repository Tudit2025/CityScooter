EAST PERTH (order 230) - references used by East1Builder (East1EastPerth.cs, East1Rail.cs)
Researched 2026-11-10. Nothing from these pages is in the repository or the game: links and notes only.

LAYOUT IN THE GAME
The shared path beside East Parade, railway on the left: the suburban island platform, then the standard gauge track
and the long terminal platform, the Public Transport Centre behind. The river opens on the right; the path goes under
the two river bridges to the power station. Optus Stadium, Matagarup Bridge, Gloucester Park and the WACA are really
1-2 km to the south-east: the stadium and the bridge are brought to the foreshore chunk at reduced size (forced
perspective), the others are left out.

NOT VERIFIED / CHOICES
- Matagarup's colours: sources conflict (some say white arches); one black and one white tube per arch is used.
- The Indian Pacific carriages' fluted sides and the coach livery are not confirmed by a text source.
- The cooling-water channel across the path (the SPLASH gap) is the game's: the power station did draw river water, but
  no channel crosses the shared path today.
- The graffiti noise wall behind the tracks is typical of the line, not a record of a particular wall (shapes only, no words).
- The steam locomotive in the centre's forecourt and the carriage inside are on the West Parade side and are not modelled.
- Animals: black swans and magpies. Jacarandas are in flower (November).
