MCIVER (order 210) - references used by East1Builder (East1Mciver.cs, East1Rail.cs)
Researched 2026-11-10. Nothing from these pages is in the repository or the game: links and notes only.
"photo" = seen in the linked picture; "text" = stated on the page.

LAYOUT IN THE GAME
Wellington St runs east with the railway on the left (north) and the hospital, Wellington Square and the Mint on the
right. Real distances are squeezed: the Mint (Hay St) and the heritage hospital buildings (Murray St) are a block south
of Wellington St in reality and stand on the street here.

NOT VERIFIED / CHOICES
- Wellington St's tree species: not found; London planes (the kit's street tree) are used.
- North Block's exact storey count and which block carries the helipad: sources disagree.
- St Mary's Cathedral and the Perth Girls School are outside the area modelled.
- Animals: Australian white ibis and pigeons (common in Perth's inner-city parks); the ambulance carries no service name.
