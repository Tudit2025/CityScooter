ELIZABETH QUAY - v0.6 polish notes (trunk B, 2026-11-11)
======================================================

Method (docs/v0.6-polish.md): a ride-through from the rider's chase camera every 20 m on the real route
(test DistrictPolishRideShots.RideThrough, -cityscooterPolishIds quay), read as contact sheets:
  before-ride-1.png, before-ride-2.png   as it was
  after-ride-1.png,  after-ride-2.png    after two rounds
16 pictures per sheet, 20 m apart; the last six are the exit, the boundary and Mounts Bay Rd's entry.
The entry after the CBD is in the CBD's ride (cbd/previews/polish) and in before/after-entry-from-cbd.png.

The ten worst things (most visible first)
-----------------------------------------
 1. Exit: hedges, a lawn and two rows of plane trees, then Mounts Bay's cliff standing in the water: the river was
    on the right here and on the left 40 m later. (before-exit-to-mounts-bay.png)
 2. Entry: the same hedge-and-lawn gap after the CBD, and beige paving starting exactly on the boundary line.
    (before-entry-from-cbd.png)
 3. Every landmark came twice: a second Bell Tower, a second arched bridge, a second Spanda in one ride.
    (before-ride-1.png, rows 1 and 4)
 4. The far scenery (CBD towers) sank at the boundary and Mounts Bay's river rose, although it is the same river.
 5. The water was another blue and 20 cm lower than Mounts Bay's.
 6. The Bell Tower's copper sails read as a tiled drum, the same height on both sides.
 7. Nothing at the south-western shore where First Contact stands; no ferry terminal as such.
 8. The plaza side had no tall lights; long stretches of promenade with only palms.
 9. Lobbies of the towers were black holes at eye level.
10. No names anywhere except the gateway.

Round 1
-------
- Exit (one riverfront): the plaza ends at a quay wall with First Contact (the bird in the boat) on its corner; from
  there the river is on BOTH sides behind the same railing; the paving hands back to asphalt and slab footpaths at a
  kerb-stone band with piano-key bars. Mounts Bay's entry carries the railing and the water on for 12 m.
- Entry (after the CBD's street): 12 m of street between two low pavilions with the CBD's plane trees, the same
  band, then the plaza on the left and the river on the right.
- One river: Mounts Bay's water level and colours; the far scenery is now Mounts Bay's prefab (the river and the
  South Perth shore), so it stays standing across the boundary. The CBD towers that were the far scenery are a third
  row in every chunk (SkylineRow).
- Bell Tower: two sails that narrow to a point with slanting tops, the one towards the city taller (about 30 m).
- Tall white light masts; lit lobbies and doors in the towers (CbdBuilder.GlassTower).

Round 2
-------
- No landmark twice: the cycle is ten chunks, the old gameplay order twice (plain, plain, the hump, plain, the jetty
  jump). Second pass: Elizabeth Quay Jetty (a longer pier under a canopy, two ferries, a QUAY JETTY board), the short
  bridge to the islet (the same hump without arches, boats on the moorings), The Landing (the carousel, The Towers
  in bronze glass). (after-jetty-second-pass.png, after-landing-second-pass.png)

Left for later
--------------
- Spanda and the Bell Tower are seen from close by; their tops leave the picture as the rider passes.
- The promenade's surface is plain between the curved bands.
- The carousel does not turn (decoration, no script).
