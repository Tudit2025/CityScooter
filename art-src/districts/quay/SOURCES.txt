ELIZABETH QUAY (district id quay, order 60) - reference sources and what was taken
=================================================================================

Reference frames were only looked at (YouTube storyboards + thumbnails, kept outside the repo in
D:\CityScooter-refs\quay\). Nothing from them is in the game: all geometry is generated by
Assets/_Project/Scripts/Editor/Districts/QuayBuilder.cs with the comic kit (ComicPalette.mat + ComicInk.mat,
vertex colours, water is a flat vertex-coloured plane). No logos or brand text; the food truck and ferry are plain.

Videos (storyboard sheets, -f sb0)

  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BGzQcRAJmbQ  (thumbnail https://i.ytimg.com/vi/BGzQcRAJmbQ/maxresdefault.jpg)

Landmarks (decoration only, no colliders)
- Elizabeth Quay Bridge: two white arches in sequence leaning out on opposite sides, hangers down to the deck edge,
  timber deck with tactile edge strips, grey mesh railings. The road goes up and over its hump (a Ramp platform).
- Bell Tower: tall glass spire (64 m + mast) with two copper sails hugging its base, on a round lawn by the water.
- Spanda: six nested white ellipse rings sharing their lowest point, 29 m tall, turned towards riders.
- The island: lawn and paving, the white kiosk with a red hipped tile roof and lantern, a playground (red tower,
  yellow slide, blue climbing frame, white shade sail), trees.
- Ferry jetty with a moored ferry; the jetty ramp before the inlet (timber, white rail posts).

Colours (sRGB hex, as used in QuayBuilder)
- Paving beige D8C7A6 / light E3D6BC, curving bands 8C877E, limestone edges ECE6D8
- Water 2F6E9A with ripple strokes 5D9AC6
- Bridge white F4F4F0, deck boards 8A5A3C, rails 9AA0A6
- Bell Tower spire glass 8FB8C8, copper B8733D / 8E5229
- Kiosk roof C4532F, lawn 6E9A45, pavilion timber A8774E
- Ferry white F4F4F0 with green 1F7A5C
- Palms: trunk 8B6B4A / 6E5238, fronds 4F8A3A / 6FA84A
- Towers on the city side: grey glass 5F7384, blue 2E5E9E, pale 7FA8D8, frames DDE3EA

Proportions
- Same street cross-section (the promenade's centre is the "road", flush-looking white limestone kerbs).
- Flatter than the street: -1.6 m per 40 m chunk (riverside).
- Bridge hump: run-up 10 m to 2.2 m, crown 8 m, down 8 m; arches 15 m high. Water 1.3 m below the promenade.
- Inlet: jetty ramp 6 m up to 1.4 m, 3 m flat jetty end, then 6 m of water across the whole promenade.
