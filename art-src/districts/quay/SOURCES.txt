ELIZABETH QUAY (district id quay, order 60) - reference sources and what was taken
=================================================================================

Reference frames were only looked at (YouTube storyboards + thumbnails, kept outside the repo in
D:\CityScooter-refs\quay\). Nothing from them is in the game: all geometry is generated by
Assets/_Project/Scripts/Editor/Districts/QuayBuilder.cs with the comic kit (ComicPalette.mat + ComicInk.mat,
vertex colours, water is a flat vertex-coloured plane). No logos or brand text; the food truck and ferry are plain.

Landmarks (decoration only, no colliders)
- Elizabeth Quay Bridge: two white arches in sequence leaning out on opposite sides, hangers down to the deck edge,
  timber deck with tactile edge strips, grey mesh railings. The road goes up and over its hump (a Ramp platform).
- Bell Tower: tall glass spire (64 m + mast) with two copper sails hugging its base, on a round lawn by the water.
- Spanda: six nested white ellipse rings sharing their lowest point, 29 m tall, turned towards riders.
- The island: lawn and paving, the white kiosk with a red hipped tile roof and lantern, a playground (red tower,
  yellow slide, blue climbing frame, white shade sail), trees.
- Ferry jetty with a moored ferry; the jetty ramp before the inlet (timber, white rail posts).

Proportions
- Same street cross-section (the promenade's centre is the "road", flush-looking white limestone kerbs).
- Flatter than the street: -1.6 m per 40 m chunk (riverside).
- Bridge hump: run-up 10 m to 2.2 m, crown 8 m, down 8 m; arches 15 m high. Water 1.3 m below the promenade.
- Inlet: jetty ramp 6 m up to 1.4 m, 3 m flat jetty end, then 6 m of water across the whole promenade.

v0.6 polish (2026-11-11): checked against the real places (web pages read, nothing copied; no images downloaded)
- Elizabeth Quay. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Quay ; DevelopmentWA's First Contact page and public art
  walk guide, via search (https://developmentwa.com.au/projects/redevelopment/elizabeth-quay/first-contact).
  Took: the inlet opens to the river at its south end; the islet at the mouth has the Florence Hummerston Kiosk
  (1927, rebuilt there) and a playground; the Elizabeth Quay Bridge spans the mouth from the islet to the western
  shore and a short bridge joins the islet to the eastern shore; Spanda (29 m) stands at The Landing on the north
  shore with a carousel beside it; the Elizabeth Quay Jetty (ferries to South Perth) is on the western shore;
  First Contact (Laurel Nannup, 5 m, cast aluminium: a bird standing in a boat) stands at the south-western shore
  by the bridge's western end; The Towers (hotel and apartments) and public moorings on the eastern shore.
  In the game: First Contact at the end of the plaza in the exit; second pass of the cycle = the jetty with its
  canopy and two ferries, the short islet bridge with moored boats, The Landing with the carousel and two
  bronze-glass towers.
- Bell Tower. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swan_Bells
  Took: 82.5 m copper-and-glass campanile at Barrack Square; a glass spire narrowing to a point; the bell chamber is
  wrapped by copper sails about 30 m tall. In the game: two sails narrowing upwards with slanting tops, one taller.
- The river and its far shore are Mounts Bay Rd's (one prefab): see art-src/districts/mountsbay/SOURCES.txt.
