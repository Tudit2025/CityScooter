UWA (id uwa, order 10): v0.6 polish notes
==========================================
Method (docs/v0.6-polish.md): ride-through from the chase camera every 20 m (test DistrictTrunkARide.RideThrough),
boundary pictures 60 / 40 / 20 m before and 0 / 20 / 40 m after (DistrictTrunkARide.Boundaries), a top ten of the
worst things, fixes from the most visible, two rounds. Compared with last night's east line
(mtlawley/previews/09-astor.png, guildford/previews/03-guildford-hotel.png).

Top ten of the worst things (before)
 1. Every arcade was a flat dark sticker on a flat wall: no depth, no columns, no shade. The whole campus read as
    cardboard next to the east line's shop fronts.
 2. The same block repeated down both sides; bare walls of one flat colour (no stone, no sills, no string course).
 3. Winthrop Hall did not match the real front: a solid box for the gallery, two tiers of belfry arches, a tall
    steep roof; a jacaranda stood in front of it and hid the pond.
 4. The entry chunk was the generic "hedge + lawn + trees" clearing: a rider coming back from Warnbro or Midland
    arrived nowhere. The exit was the same chunk mirrored.
 5. UWA -> Matilda Bay: the campus stopped in a lawn, then a different place began; paving cut from campus
    limestone to grey slabs and a red path exactly on the boundary line; the far scenery sank and another rose.
 6. The far scenery was rows of pale boxes with red lids to the horizon on both sides (a toy town).
 7. Roofs were bare single-colour wedges.
 8. The reflection pond was the same light blue as the lily pond, with lilies; the real one is dark and still.
 9. No Moreton Bay fig anywhere; the peacocks only appeared as random pedestrians.
10. Rows of identical jacarandas, all one size.

Round 1
 1. CampusBlock rebuilt: a real ground-floor arcade (TrunkAKit.Arcade: round arches with reveals on stout round
    columns with capitals and bases, a paved walk 1.6 m deep, the ground-floor wall with doors and windows in the
    shade behind it), the upper floor over the walk.
 2. Rock-faced limestone: blocks in two tones on the walls; sills under the upper windows; string course.
 3. Winthrop Hall rebuilt from photographs (SOURCES.txt): undercroft of 12 arches on round columns, loggia of 18
    small arches in threes with a balustrade, six tall narrow windows, the east pavilion, a low tile roof; tower
    with one tier of four open belfry arches a side, a pair of windows, the blue-and-cream clock under a hood, a low
    pyramid roof, the small balcony. The tree in front is gone.
 4. Entry: the street arrives at a campus gate (limestone pylons with terracotta caps and wing walls, cypress
    pairs, street trees from the street kit before the gate); grey paving turns to campus limestone behind a brick
    band at the gate.
 5. Exit: the campus edge carries on along the left; on the right the last block ends, the lawn opens to peppermint
    trees, beach and Matilda Bay with moored yachts; the red shared path starts inside this chunk (stone band, bar,
    SHARED PATH on the path); a HACKETT DR street sign. Matilda Bay's entry starts with the same cross-section.
    One far-scenery prefab for UWA and Matilda Bay (PRP_Crawley_FarRiverside): it no longer sinks at the boundary.
 6. Far scenery: the river and the far shore on the right (the campus is on Matilda Bay), campus roofs among
    two-tone trees and the Kings Park ridge on the left; the chunks' own far blocks got arched windows.
 7. TileRoof: two darker tile courses a slope and hip ridges.
 8. Reflection pond: dark still water nearly flush with the paving, the hall and tower as pale streaks in it,
    stone bench walls at the ends, no lilies.
 9. A Moreton Bay fig beside Winthrop Hall (towards the Arts building) and one behind the palms of the grove;
    peacocks on the lawn of the cloister quad (scenery, out of reach).

Round 2
10. Jacarandas in two sizes in the grove, arcades and Winthrop chunks; one palm less in the grove (budget).
 -  Checked the ride again: nothing floats or pokes through; triangle counts 13,988 to 24,900 a chunk (limit 25,000).

Gameplay layout: unchanged (lanes, slots, the steps-and-pond jump, benches / bike racks as sidewalk obstacles at
the same places, obstacle and pedestrian lists, length).

Left weak
- Cloisters is at 24,900 triangles: no room for more there.
- The upper-floor windows are still flat (dark arch on the wall, with a sill); only the ground-floor arcades and
  Winthrop Hall's galleries and belfry have real depth.
- The tower keeps a clock on three faces (in life the east face has none): a rider approaches from that side and
  should see "the clock tower".
- The hall is 34 m long (64 m in life) to fit one 40 m chunk; the tower is 44 m (49 m).
