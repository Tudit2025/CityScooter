PERTH CBD (district id cbd, order 50) - reference sources and what was taken
=========================================================================

Reference frames were only looked at (YouTube storyboards + thumbnails, kept outside the repo in
D:\CityScooter-refs\cbd\). Nothing from them is in the game: all geometry is generated by
Assets/_Project/Scripts/Editor/Districts/CbdBuilder.cs with the comic kit (ComicPalette.mat + ComicInk.mat,
vertex colours). No logos, brand names or shop names anywhere; signs are plain colour bands.

Videos (storyboard sheets, -f sb0)
- Driving in Perth: Driving Through Perth City Centre (Western Australia) - 4K
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ja--f3D5tE
  Took: glass tower canyon of St Georges Terrace, deep blue / teal / pale silver glass, towers with podium lobbies,
  plane trees (bare in winter, green in the game), towers set back behind forecourts.
- St. George Terrace Perth CBD, Western Australia [4K]
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DmATYQg-Dg  (thumbnail https://i.ytimg.com/vi/4DmATYQg-Dg/maxresdefault.jpg)
  Took: red-brick heritage buildings with cream stone trim and slate roofs between glass towers, the cream concrete
  tower with dark ribbon windows, white-banded office slab, traffic light poles at every corner.
- Exploring Perth Australia: Walking Tour Along Hay Street Mall 2025
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zYyACGpUtL4
  Took: Hay Street Mall - grey pavers wall to wall, low granite edge instead of a kerb, two- to four-storey pale
  heritage facades with tall sash windows and parapets, continuous dark awnings over the shopfronts, plane trees,
  benches, kiosks and planters in the mall, lots of people, no cars.
- Searched but not used further: SpEtHwxC4pI, hMyZ5IvLaGU (St Georges Terrace walks), V__hRXcCbPo (Murray / Hay St).

Landmarks (stylised, recognisable shapes only)
- Town hall clock tower: red brick, white trim bands and quoins, arched belfry openings, clock faces, steep slate
  spire with four pinnacles (Perth Town Hall, corner of Hay and Barrack St).
- Mock-Tudor arcade entrance: white render, dark timber framing with diagonal braces, jettied upper floors, gable,
  arched entrance with a clock above it (London Court).
- Sandstone colonnade with a heavy cornice and slate mansard (Old Treasury / GPO family on the Terrace).
- The tallest tower: pale silver glass, dark frames, stepped crown and an antenna (Central Park-style).
- Dark glass tower with big white diagonal braces (Brookfield Place-style).

  awnings 3A3F46, facades EFE6D2 E9D3B8 D8E0E4 F3EBDD E4C9A8 D7CFC2

Proportions
- Street cross-section unchanged (8 m road, 4 m sidewalks, 0.2 m kerbs, 40 m chunks, standard 4.5 degree downhill).
- Towers 40-150 m (floor bands every 3.6 m, 6.5 m lobby podium); heritage 2-4 storeys at 4-4.4 m; mall shops
  4.2 m ground floor + 3.6 m upper floors, awnings at 3.35-3.6 m reaching 2.5 m over the sidewalk.
- Town hall clock tower 30 m + 9 m spire; clock faces 1.35 m radius at 15.8 m.
