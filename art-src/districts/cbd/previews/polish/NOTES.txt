PERTH CBD - v0.6 polish notes (trunk B, 2026-11-11)
=================================================

Method (docs/v0.6-polish.md): a ride-through from the rider's chase camera every 20 m on the real route
(test DistrictPolishRideShots.RideThrough, -cityscooterPolishIds "cbd>quay,cbd>mciver@-100"), read as contact sheets:
  before-ride-1..3.png   as it was (the ride that turns to Elizabeth Quay)
  after-ride-1..3.png    after two rounds
16 pictures per sheet, 20 m apart; the last six are the exit, the boundary and Elizabeth Quay's entry.
Both continuations: before/after-exit-to-quay.png and before/after-exit-to-mciver.png; from West Perth:
before/after-entry-from-west-perth.png.

The ten worst things (most visible first)
-----------------------------------------
 1. The whole district was in shadow: the sun stands behind and to the left of the rider, and the towers on the left
    kept the road, both footpaths and the mall dark from the first chunk to the last. Next to last night's sunlit
    Beaufort Street the CBD looked like a winter evening. (before-ride-1.png)
 2. Entry and exit were hedge-and-lawn gaps with rows of plane trees: a park between West Perth's street and the
    city, and again before the quay or McIver. (before-entry-from-west-perth.png, before-exit-to-*.png)
 3. The Town Hall could not be seen coming: its tower stood behind the building line, hidden by a 70 m glass tower
    until the rider was beside it. (before-town-hall.png)
 4. Town Hall and the tallest towers came twice in one ride (the cycle was eight chunks, the district is 480-640 m).
 5. Eye level was dark lobby glass, tower after tower; no colour, no names.
 6. Hay Street Mall: dark grey pavers, every awning the same charcoal, black shop windows. (before-hay-st-mall.png)
 7. London Court was one 8 m front with a small gable, easy to miss.
 8. One plane tree stamped along every kerb, all the same size.
 9. The Town Hall had round-headed windows and a closed ground floor: the real one stands on pointed arches.
10. No street names: nothing says St Georges Terrace or Hay Street.

Round 1
-------
- Sunlit street: while the CBD's own chunks are built, the buildings on the left go into the chunk's Landmark
  renderer (ink outline, receives but does not cast shadows). Trees, lamps, banners, awnings and traffic still throw
  shadows; the mall's awnings always cast theirs. 8 renderers per chunk (the budget).
- Entry: West Perth's street runs on: two-storey shops under awnings from z = 0, three-storey heritage fronts, then
  the first towers. No hedge, no lawn.
- Exit: towers, heritage fronts, then two- and three-storey fronts under awnings to the end, street trees and lamps:
  the same city street before the quay and before McIver.
- Town Hall: the corner at the crossing is an open square with a low sandstone building at its back, the hall and
  its tower start 14 m on, so the tower shows from the chunk before. The building follows the real one: an open
  undercroft of pointed arches, tall pointed hall windows, buttresses, a steep slate roof with dormers, the corner
  tower with clock faces towards the rider and the road, pointed belfry openings, four corner turrets and a spire.
- London Court: 12 m wide, three gables (the middle one taller) with bargeboards and flags, the oriel with the clock,
  heraldic shields with red crosses, the arch with a wrought-iron gate, LONDON COURT over it.
- Mall: lighter pavers, five awning colours, lit display windows, tree sizes varied, a HAY ST MALL street sign.
- Terrace: banner poles, street signs ST GEORGES TCE and BARRACK ST (5-wide M, N, W), lit lobbies and doors and a
  coloured canopy edge on every tower, plane trees in five sizes.

Round 2
-------
- No landmark twice: the cycle is sixteen chunks with exactly the old order of chunk types (so the district ends
  where it always did). The second time, St George's Cathedral (brick nave, steep roof, gabled front with a tall
  pointed window, castellated bell tower) stands in the Town Hall's place, the plaza has other towers, and the mall
  chunk without London Court comes first. (after-cathedral-second-pass.png)

Left for later
--------------

- The glass towers are one recipe in different colours and crowns; two or three more silhouettes would help.
- Murray Street Mall is not there as its own place (the mall chunks are Hay Street).
