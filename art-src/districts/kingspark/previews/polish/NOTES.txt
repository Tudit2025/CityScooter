Top ten of the worst things (before)
 1. Kings Park -> West Perth: the avenue ended in shrubs and lawn with West Perth's huge blank back blocks standing
    in the open on both sides; then a wall of shop fronts started on the line.
 2. Matilda Bay -> Kings Park: hedge blobs, a lawn gap, then the gums.
 3. The left side showed the sky's river band under the trees: the avenue looked like a ridge with water on both
    sides (the park's interior is on the left in life).
 4. No State War Memorial at all (the lookout was a wall and two coin binoculars).
 5. Beside the Federation Walkway the camera looked at one huge flat grey slab (the deck's edge beam at eye level).
 6. The walkway's steel was grey; it is rust-coloured weathering steel.
 7. The KINGS PARK name wall and the FEDERATION WALKWAY board used the 3-pixel N and W.
 8. The entry was calm for 13 m (clipped shrubs) before anything of the park showed.
 9. Nothing marked the Fraser Avenue end of the park (no gate, no clock).
10. The same gum in the same rhythm in every chunk; little under them in places.

Round 1
 1. Exit: the gums end at a park gate (limestone piers with ball caps, z = 20); beyond it a paved forecourt, a
    street tree from the street kit, the Edith Cowan memorial clock (background, behind the kerb, no collider), and
    four buildings generated with the street kit (cafe and office on the left, row house and bank on the right) on
    the line of West Perth's shop fronts. They also hide West Perth's blank back blocks.
 2. Entry: the retaining wall and bush bank of Matilda Bay's exit step down to the park's low wall by z = 14, gums
    from the first metre, grass tree and banksia on the bank, the name wall on the right. No hedges.
 3. Far view: on the left ground, a wall of gum crowns on pale trunks and a darker mass behind, to the horizon.
 4. The State War Memorial at the lookout: the grey granite obelisk with its cross on a stepped podium with white
    rails and two flagpoles, on a limestone terrace at the edge of the scarp, two palms. Background only: beyond
    the balustrade, no collider.
 6. Walkway steel in rust colours (deck edge beams, arch, columns).
 7. Both signs in TrunkAKit's pixel font (5-wide M, N, W, V, Y).
