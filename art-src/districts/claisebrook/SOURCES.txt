CLAISEBROOK (order 220) - references used by East1Builder (East1Claisebrook.cs, East1Rail.cs)
Researched 2026-11-10. Nothing from these pages is in the repository or the game: links and notes only.
"photo" = seen in the linked picture; "text" = stated on the page.

LAYOUT IN THE GAME
The station and depot are on the left; Royal St and the cove follow (the railway goes under a road bridge first). In
reality the cove is a few hundred metres east of the line; here it is the next block.

Station and depot
1. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claisebrook_railway_station

Claisebrook Cove
9. https://trailswa.com.au/storage/media/n0742ope5dzx/Claisebrook-Cove-Village-Art-Trail.pdf
   text and photos: the cove's shape (a narrow mouth on the Swan between two grassy headlands, Victoria Gardens to the
   south; the channel ends near a small round lake); the Victoria Gardens Shelter with its red-and-white striped wavy
   roof on steel arches; the Impossible Triangle (13.5 m) at the gateway to East Perth -> RoundLake, the canal chunk,
   VictoriaShelter; the triangle stands in EAST PERTH's entry chunk.
10. https://scoopwebsite.blob.core.windows.net/userfiles/file/EPRA%20Claisebrook%20Brochure%20Web.pdf
   text and photos: an inlet made from the old city drain, with moorings and jetties; pale limestone block walls, cream
   paving, tapered pale bollards with inset lights, timber boardwalks for alfresco dining, black lamp posts, white
   cafe umbrellas; small motor cruisers and dinghies; two and three storey townhouses in cream, ochre, peach and
   terracotta render (the odd teal one), terracotta or dark grey tiled gables, arched windows, black metal balconies;
   four and five storey cream apartment blocks with glass balconies near the mouth; "deciduous trees line the streets"
   -> CoveSide, CoveBollard, CoveJetty, Cruiser, Boardwalk, TownhouseRow / Townhouse, CoveApartments, plane trees.
11. https://yoursay.perth.wa.gov.au/trafalgar-bridge-rejuvenation-project
   text: Trafalgar Bridge, built 1994, timber deck (re-decked 2020).
12. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Claisebrook_Cove,_East_Perth,_January_2021_08.jpg
   photo: Trafalgar Bridge: two towers, each a pair of dark cylindrical masts with cross-braces; straight white stays
   fanning to a slender level deck; a white balustrade; a Norfolk Island pine beside it -> TrafalgarBridge, the pine

13. https://inherit.dplh.wa.gov.au/Public/Inventory/PrintSingleRecord/ecdaed3e-9a7a-421d-81f9-34106f840cc4

14. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_public_art_in_Western_Australia

15. https://mail.perthwalkabout.com/Local-Area-Guide/claisebrook-cove-east-perth

NOT VERIFIED / CHOICES
- Curved corrugated roofs on some townhouses and on the apartments' lift core: one curved-roof building shows in the
  aerial; used as one of three roof types.
- The three white needle masts near the footbridge in photo 4 could not be identified and are left out.
- The canal crossing: the real promenade crosses the canal on a small footbridge; the missing middle (SPLASH) is the game's.
- Trafalgar Bridge's length and height were not found; it spans the 40 m cove of the chunk.
- Animals: Pacific black ducks and a pied cormorant (both common on the cove).
