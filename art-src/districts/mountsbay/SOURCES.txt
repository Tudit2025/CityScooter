General knowledge of the place (no image copied):
- Riding from the city back west: Kings Park escarpment on the right (stepped pale limestone #E3D4A8 / #D6C294 / #C2AD80,
  bush on the ledges, gums on top), Swan River on the left (water #3379B3, light #619FD1, sparkles #C7E6F5), rock revetment
  (#A8A191), yachts, the South Perth shore far across.
- Near the city end: palm-lined frontage and apartment towers; the Narrows Bridge / freeway deck crossing high over the
  road on concrete piers (#CCC7BA, undersides #8C8A85); Jacob's Ladder - the long steep staircase up the cliff with dark
  green handrails and people climbing it.
- A small inlet crossing (game invention): full-width limestone river-wall ramp, 5 m of water (SPLASH).
- Australian pelican: white, black wings, huge pale pink bill. Perth wheelie bin: dark green body, yellow recycling lid.

Proportions used: road 8 m + 4 m paths (contract), retaining wall 3 m high at x = +8.35 m, cliff up to 16-30 m from x = 12 m,
water 1.1 m below the road, Narrows deck 9.4 m above the road, 15 m wide, piers outside |x| > 10 m.

v0.6 polish (2026-11-11): checked against the real places (web pages read, nothing copied; no images downloaded)
- Old Swan Brewery. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swan_Brewery ; inHerit (Heritage Council of WA) records of the
  Old Swan Brewery precinct and its former stables, via search.
  Took: "red brick and tile Federation style"; the 1879 building is the core of the complex, a landmark seen from the
  Narrows and from Kings Park; a tall, high-roofed structure with turrets; a central lift and staircase tower on the
  Mounts Bay Road face; the car park on the other side of the road stands where the stables were; Kennedy's Fountain,
  a fresh-water spring, is at the foot of the Mount Eliza escarpment nearby.
  In the game: gabled tile roofs, cross gables, string courses, arched windows, the stair tower with turrets, a brick
  chimney, the footbridge to car-park decks in the escarpment. The name wall says OLD BREWERY (no brand name).
- Narrows Bridge. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrows_Bridge_(Perth)
  Took: two road bridges with a railway bridge between them (2005); arched along its length (a vertical curve), five
  spans, footpaths on the outer sides behind light aluminium balustrades with the lights built into the fence line;
  links the Mitchell and Kwinana freeways between Point Lewis (at the foot of Mount Eliza) and Mill Point.
  In the game (the exit): limestone abutment, balustrade and lamp standards on the river side, the freeway and the
  railway with gantries on the other side, level water under the bridge, boards KWINANA FWY and NARROWS BRIDGE.
- Mounts Bay Road shared path: City of Perth project page, via search (the red path along the river is the main
  east-west cycling route between Crawley and the city).
- General knowledge of the road, stylised: the Convention Centre at the city end; the State War Memorial obelisk on
  the edge of Mount Eliza above the road; the Kokoda Track Memorial Walk climbing from Kennedy Fountain to the park;
  Jacob's Ladder; Eliza, the bronze diver on a pile in the river at the old Crawley Baths, whom locals dress up;
  black swans; South Perth's apartment towers and Norfolk pines across the water.
